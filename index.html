About Photo Factory Nepal

The People Behind the Moments

Photo Factory Nepal is a Kathmandu-based team of photographers and videographers. We believe the best wedding stories are told quietly — by paying attention, staying close to the people who matter, and letting real emotion lead.

From the first ritual to the last dance, our goal is simple: images and films that feel like your day, not a performance of it.
